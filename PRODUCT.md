# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Friends and small communities who want a straightforward place for private, one-to-one conversations.

## Product Purpose

Chattap lets people create an account and exchange direct messages in real time, with image sharing, presence awareness, and personal theme choices. The landing page should help first-time visitors understand the experience and confidently create an account.

## Positioning

A personal chat app where an account unlocks immediate, live one-to-one conversations with other registered people, rather than a public feed or group workspace.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive unauthenticated at the landing page, can try a simulated conversation and switch themes, then can sign up or log in. Authenticated users use the application to find contacts, see live availability, and exchange messages and images.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Real-time one-to-one messaging and online presence use Socket.IO.
- Users can share images, customize a profile avatar, and choose from 32 DaisyUI themes.
- The landing page must retain its interactive chat demonstration and live theme picker.
- The current React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and DaisyUI stack remains in place.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Chattap. The landing page should avoid generic AI-generated visual tropes and must not make unverified performance, commercial, or security claims.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains a working interactive chat simulation and theme switcher in `frontend/src/pages/LandingPage.jsx`. No customer testimonials, usage figures, pricing, or third-party endorsements are available to use as proof.

## Product Principles

1. Make the private conversation experience understandable before asking for sign-up.
2. Treat live presence and real-time exchange as product behavior to demonstrate, not hype to claim.
3. Keep customization playful but subordinate to starting a conversation.
4. Make account creation the clearest next step for an unauthenticated visitor.
